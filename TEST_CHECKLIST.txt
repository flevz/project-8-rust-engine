This test build: grinds (translated from the original), plus the known-issues list.
How to grind: roll at a rail (not a ramp's edge), ollie, and hold Y (Triangle) as you pass over it.
Things to look for:
 1. Grab: the skater snaps onto the rail and slides along it, a little faster than before (the rail boost).
 2. Which grind: the direction you hold when you land on the rail picks it, as in the original:
      nothing = 50-50, Up = Nosegrind, Down = 5-0, Left / Right = slides (Lipslide, Noseslide ...).
    Landing across the rail gives a boardslide or lipslide.
 3. Up, Up, then Y in the air just before the rail: Nosebluntslide (other combos from the original lists too).
 4. Balance meter: it shows on screen while grinding. Left / Right lean you back. It gets harder the
    longer you grind. If it tips over you drop off the side (the bail after that is not done yet).
 5. Corners and joins: the grind carries on round gentle corners and along joined rail pieces.
    A sharp corner (more than 50 degrees) or the end of the rail drops you off into the air.
 6. Ollie (release A) while grinding: you jump off the rail and land normally.
 7. Grinding downhill speeds you up, uphill slows you.
 8. Something in the way on the rail knocks you off into the air.
 9. Single posts that count as rails (z_houses has four, in the lower bowl area): grabbing one does a
    Natas Spin on top of it. Hold RB (R1) as you grab it to spin the other way. Ollie to leave.
10. RT (R2) held as you land across a rail: a stall (you stop on the rail); on a steep rail you slide off it.
    (Not tried in the headless test; tell me what happens.)
11. Lips (Y at the top of a vert ramp) should work exactly as before.
Not done yet (see docs/KNOWN_ISSUES.md): the grind camera (it acts as on the ground), grind sparks and
sounds, the score, the bail when you fall off a rail, moving rails, bails, wallrides.
